\at{12}{107}{131}{\bodyfont\sect{01}{Background \& objective}
Atrial fibrillation (AF) is associated with stroke risk and may be unrecognized [1]. ECG models can encounter different patients, acquisition conditions and labels at a new site. We ask whether a model trained on two source domains retains discrimination and useful uncertainty on a third. The task is \textbf{AF versus explicit sinus rhythm (SR)}, not all-arrhythmia diagnosis.}

\at{12}{207}{131}{\bodyfont\sect{03}{Deep learning \& evaluation protocol}
Signals are ordered into 12 canonical leads, converted to mV and resampled to \textbf{100 Hz for 10 seconds}. Per-lead normalization uses training data only. Compact ResNet and InceptionTime-style CNNs are compared with empirical risk minimization, class-conditional feature alignment and CORAL [4,6]. Source validation selects ResNet with alignment weight 0.05. Three seeds (42, 202, 3407) form the exported ensemble.}

\at{12}{310}{131}{\bodyfont\sect{04}{Calibration \& software implementation}
Source-only temperature scaling [5] precedes probability averaging. A frozen threshold of \textbf{0.6211} gives the AF/SR label; predictive entropy flags review. The source goal of 97\% sensitivity at 95\% specificity per domain was \textbf{not met} (minimum sensitivity: 95.31\%).

The \textbf{Streamlit app} validates 100/500 Hz numerical ECG uploads and displays waveforms, predictions, model comparisons, calibration and robustness analyses. Thirteen software checks passed, including both exported ensembles and upload-to-result inference.}

\at{153}{330}{132}{\bodyfont\sect{07}{Interpretation \& limitations}
Repeated Georgia evaluation is \textbf{exploratory} after prior-result inspection. Only one external rotation is complete; Shaoxing/Georgia IDs are patient proxies, and near duplicates may remain. Alignment does not dominate all metrics. Intervals condition on fitted models (200 cluster-bootstrap replicates). Independent cohorts and prospective workflow validation are required.}
